Little Hands Fast Cars: Coloring Book

For children fascinated by racing cars and fast vehicles, this paperback includes real car illustrations, colour-dot guidance and progressively more detailed activities.

Start with three coloured-dot cues in Level 1. Later levels offer four, five and six cues; children can choose their own colours for unmarked parts.

Garage reward pages mark level completion. The highest level features six colour cues plus black as a surprise seventh colour.
