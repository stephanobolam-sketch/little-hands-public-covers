A free vehicle colour-spotting activity

Try this simple colouring idea with a vehicle outline and three crayons or pencils. No purchase is required.

What you need

Choose any vehicle outline picture, red, blue and yellow pencils or crayons, and any other colours your child wants to use. An adult can draw the outline or help mark the colour clues.

Spot three colours, then invent the rest

  1. Choose a favourite vehicle: a digger, fire truck, bus, car or aircraft.
  2. Add three colour clues: an adult puts a small red, blue and yellow dot on three different parts of the picture.
  3. Find and match: spot each coloured dot together and colour that section to match.
  4. Make the rest your own: choose colours freely for the unmarked parts. Ask which part was easiest to spot or which colour to use next.
  5. Celebrate the picture: display the finished drawing somewhere your child likes.

For a bigger challenge, try four, five or six marked colours. Follow your child's interest and pace.
