How color-by-dots works

  1. Spot the coloured dots on parts of the vehicle.
  2. Match those marked parts to their dots.
  3. Colour the unmarked parts however you like.
  4. Try the next level when ready, and celebrate with completion rewards.

Level 1 starts with three colour-dot cues; later levels build to four, five and six. Fast Cars adds black as a surprise seventh colour in its final level. Adults can help with cutting the garage and hangar reward pages.

For parents, grandparents and preschool educators
