Construction vehicle coloring book for kids

Little Hands Big Machines

For children fascinated by trucks, diggers, cranes and big working machines, this paperback offers vehicle colouring pages with simple colour clues and room to choose their own colours.

Start with three coloured-dot cues in Level 1. Later levels offer four, five and six cues; children can choose their own colours for unmarked parts.

Garage reward pages celebrate completing each level. Adults can help with cut-out rewards.
